5.What you are responsible for

The software is built so that publishing waits for a human decision. From there, the responsibilities are yours:

  • Every post is approved by you before it goes out, and what you approve is your content, published from your account.
  • Verify before you publish. The AI drafts and researches, but research can be wrong, and a claim about a product becomes yours once you approve it.
  • Follow Meta's rules — the Meta Platform Terms and the terms that come with the Threads product — along with the terms of the other services you connect.
  • Follow the law that applies to you. Affiliate content can carry advertising-disclosure or endorsement requirements where you live, and the plugin deliberately does not decide that for you.
  • Keep the products and links in your sheet legitimate: your affiliate relationships, your URLs, and the claims you bring.

6.Acceptable use

Use the software for its purpose: drafting and publishing affiliate content you are willing to stand behind. In practice, that rules out:

  • Spam, bulk unsolicited posting, and attempts to game Threads engagement or reach.
  • Content Meta's terms prohibit, or publishing in a way those terms prohibit.
  • Invented first-hand claims. The pipeline refuses experience claims your own stored testimony does not support; if you modify the code, do not use that freedom to publish claims you cannot back.
  • Publishing without the approval step. The pipeline is built so a human decides what goes out; if you remove that, the decisions become yours alone.
